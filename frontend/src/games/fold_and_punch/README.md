# Fold & Punch

**Measures:** spatial thinking.

**Paradigm:** Paper folding (as in the ETS Kit of Factor-Referenced Cognitive Tests, Ekstrom et al., 1976). Written from published descriptions; every item is made in code.

**What you do:** a square of paper is folded one to three times (the shaded half folds over), then a hole is punched through every layer. Pick the unfolded paper from four.

**Metrics:** `accuracy`, `median_rt_ms` (right answers). Wrong options are the punch alone, the folds mirrored across the wrong axis, a missed fold, or a hole moved.

**Difficulty knobs:** `folds` 1 to 3.
