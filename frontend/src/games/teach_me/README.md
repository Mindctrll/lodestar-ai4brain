# Teach Me

**Measures:** verbal skill (primary), reasoning; ACS clarification.

**Paradigm:** Original game (docs/plan/06, 11).

**What you do:** Lodestar is confused about something; explain it in your own words. One request per round is ambiguous (two things named, then "it"): asking what Lodestar means is the right move.

**Metrics:** `rubric_score`: the share of the topic's key ideas mentioned, scaled down for very short answers (scored in code; low weight). ACS clarification as in Guide the Robot.

**Difficulty knobs:** `topic_difficulty` 0.2 to 0.9 (topics are tagged by difficulty in config.yaml).
