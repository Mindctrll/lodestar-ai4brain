# Card Gamble

**Measures:** decision style under risk (shown as a style).

**Paradigm:** Iowa Gambling Task idea (Bechara et al., 1994). Written from published descriptions; PEBL has a version, used for ideas only.

**What you do:** pick 40 cards from four decks. A and B win more per card but lose more over time; C and D win less but come out ahead. The decks aren't labelled as good or bad: you learn them as you go.

**Metrics:** `advantageous_choices` (picks from C and D minus picks from A and B), `late_advantageous` (the same over the second half), `points`. Not right or wrong: no ability score.

**Difficulty knobs:** none.
