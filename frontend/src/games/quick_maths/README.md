# Quick Maths

**Measures:** numerical thinking (primary), processing speed.

**Paradigm:** Arithmetic fluency (as in the Woodcock-Johnson Math Fluency test). Written from published descriptions.

**What you do:** solve each sum fast and press Enter.

**Metrics:** `correct_per_min`, accuracy.

**Difficulty knobs:** `operations` 1 to 4 (+, then -, then ×, then ÷), `operand_max` 10 to 100. Also plays in the terminal (`cli/games.py`).
