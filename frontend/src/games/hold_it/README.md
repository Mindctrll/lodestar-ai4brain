# Hold It

**Measures:** inhibition (primary).

**Paradigm:** go/no-go, as in the jspsych-timelines go/no-go (MIT). Our own UI.

**What you do:** shapes appear one at a time. Press Space (or tap Go) for every circle; hold back
when the shape is a hexagon marked "Hold".

**Metrics:** commission errors (presses on Hold shapes), median go RT, accuracy.

**Difficulty knobs:** `go_ratio` 0.6 to 0.9 (rarer holds are harder to stop for), `isi_ms` 1200
to 500 (the pace).
