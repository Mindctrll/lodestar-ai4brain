# Model card: Engagement Risk

Generated by `backend/ml/train_engagement.py` (version 202610211349). Do not edit by hand; run `make ml-engagement`.

## What it predicts

The chance that a person has no activity at all in the next 7 days, as a range (inductive Venn-Abers). Najma uses it to offer a shorter, easier session to someone who is drifting away. It is never shown as a score of the person.

## Data

OULAD (Kuzilek et al. 2017, *Scientific Data*), CC BY 4.0: 25,273 students in 22 course presentations, daily activity summaries. One example per enrolment and course week while the student is registered: 512,871 train, 169,603 calibration and 171,912 test examples, split by student. A week off happens in 36% of examples.

Features are behaviour only, the ones Najma has too: days since last activity, active days in the last 7 days, the 7 before and the last 28, the trend in activity per active day, the current streak, failures in the last 28 days and days since starting. **No demographics** (gender, age, region, disability and deprivation are left out).

## Limits

- OULAD is about online courses, not brain games. This is a transfer of behaviour patterns, and it has not been validated on Najma users yet; that needs pilot data.
- "Activity" is clicks in OULAD and rounds in Najma. The trend feature is a ratio, so the scale doesn't matter, but habits around a course and a game differ.
- A course has deadlines and an end; practice doesn't.

## Must not be used for

Judging, ranking or comparing people, streaks or guilt messages, or anything clinical. Only to make a session shorter and easier, or to offer one friendly nudge.
